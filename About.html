ABOUT MEDLOGE-ENUGU

Find a place to live. Make a confident decision.

Medloge-Enugu is being built to make it easier for students, medical professionals and other people around UNTH to discover accommodation and understand their options before making a decision.

OUR PURPOSE

Why Medloge exists

Finding accommodation can involve searching through different places, comparing options, contacting people and trying to arrange inspections.

Medloge brings these steps into one simple experience so users can discover houses, view property information, save options and request inspections.

HOW IT WORKS

From search to inspection

01

Find a house

Search through available accommodation and explore property details.

02

Compare your options

Look at property information, facilities, photos and other available details.

03

Request an inspection

Choose a preferred inspection date and method before making a rental decision.

TRANSPARENCY

Information should be clear.

Medloge is designed around clear property information, visible details and an inspection-first approach.

Real property information

Listings should clearly show the information available about a property.

Clear rental details

Pricing and important property conditions should be communicated clearly.

Inspect before deciding

Users can request an inspection before making a rental decision or payment.
