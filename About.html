About Medloge-Enugu

For years, our beautiful country has produced nurses, doctors, pharmacists and many other healthcare professionals.

But another important question is: Where will they work?

Medloge-Enugu is being built to make finding accommodation easier for students and young professionals around UNTH.

Instead of spending hours searching for houses, dealing with unnecessary stress or travelling from one place to another, Medloge brings available accommodation together in one place.

What We Do

Medloge helps users discover accommodation, view important house information, save houses they like and request inspections.

We want users to have useful information before they decide to visit or rent a house.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build more than a house-renting platform.

As Medloge grows, we want to connect students, healthcare professionals, businesses and useful services in one ecosystem.

We also hope to collaborate with hospitals, organizations and other partners in Nigeria and eventually beyond Nigeria.
